Restaurant Services

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air outside all shift long, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without throwing off the building's pressure. Malden HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. If a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, drags hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and wears out your staff during the rush. Our technicians balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and guests comfortable throughout.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Malden HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and auditors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

An HVAC emergency on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, empty a retail floor on a summer afternoon, overheat a server room, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Malden HVAC provides fast, emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Malden and surrounding areas. When you call our commercial line, we dispatch an experienced technician quickly, because every hour without climate control costs you money.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. Whatever it is, we find it fast, get the building comfortable again, and document the entire call for your facility files.
